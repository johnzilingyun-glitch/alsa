## Audit Protocol 2: 🧮 Valuation Normalization & WACC White-boxing

1. **WACC derivation** (no black-box allowed):
   - $R_f$: Current 10Y sovereign yield (CGB10Y for China, US10Y for US). Use `web_search` to find "US 10Y Treasury yield latest site:cnbc.com OR site:bloomberg.com OR site:investing.com" or "中国10年期国债收益率 最新 site:investing.com OR site:tradingeconomics.com OR site:finance.sina.com.cn".
   - $\beta$: 2-year regression beta vs benchmark
   - $ERP$: A-shares 5-7%, US 4-6%
   - $WACC$: When evaluating discount rate, WACC must consider interest-bearing debt: if debt ratio is very low (<15%), WACC can use CAPM directly: WACC ≈ Rf + β × ERP; if debt ratio is significant (≥15%), must use weighted formula: WACC = (E/V) * (Rf + β × ERP) + (D/V) * Kd * (1 - Tc), where Kd is estimated as: Financial expenses / Total interest-bearing debt; tax rate Tc is 15% or 25% by default.
   - **Audit red line**: If analyst WACC < derived WACC - 1%, flag as "WACC too low, valuation inflated"
   - **White-box Disclosure Audit**: Check if the analyst or strategist report explicitly discloses $R_f$, $\beta$, $ERP$, $K_d$, $T_c$, and weights. If missing, flag as `[🚨 Valuation Blackbox: Missing WACC Parameter Derivation]`.

2. **DCF Modeling Validity Audit**:
   - FCF growth must align with industry growth + market share gain logic.
   - In commodity surplus cycles: prohibit >10% long-term FCF growth assumption.
   - Terminal growth $g$ must be restricted and strictly less than WACC: US shares $g$ range 1.5% - 2.5%, hard cap 3.0%; A/HK shares $g$ range 2.0% - 3.0%, hard cap 3.5%.
   - **Terminal Value (TV) Proportion Sensitivity Audit**: Calculate the proportion of Terminal Value to total Enterprise Value. If TV $\ge 80\%$, must output `[⚠️ DCF High Sensitivity Warning: Terminal Value accounts for XX% of total EV]`, highlighting that the valuation is highly dependent on long-term terminal assumptions with immense uncertainty, and mandate a supplementary SOTP valuation for validation.
   - **FCF Turnaround Quality Audit**: Dissect the source of FCF turnaround. If it is driven by CAPEX reduction rather than organic OCF growth, warn of the risk of capacity stagnation and the onset of a reinvestment cycle; forbid linear extrapolation of long-term high FCF growth.

3. **ROE DuPont decomposition**:
   - If ROE(reported) - ROE(DuPont) > 5%: disclose source of gap
   - Check: minority interest leverage effect
   - If confirmed: **mandate downside stress test for cycle reversal**

4. **Management Behavior & Buyback Timeliness Audit**:
   - If thesis = "severely undervalued" but management is doing M&A/dilution instead of buybacks, apply 5-8% governance/agency discount.
   - **Buyback Timeliness Audit**: Verify the status of buyback programs. If the referenced buybacks are already fully executed/completed at the current date, forbid listing them as future catalysts or buying support. Flag as `[⚠️ Stale Catalyst: Buyback already completed, marginal buying support has expired]`.

5. **SOTP mandatory output** (Sum-of-the-Parts as safety margin anchor)
6. **PEG Calculation Consistency Audit**:
    - Audit the PEG calculation logic to ensure no mismatch between dynamic PE and historical growth.
    - If a mismatch is detected (e.g. Forward PE divided by historical 3Y CAGR), flag as `[🚨 PEG Mismatch: Mixed Trailing/Forward Components]` and enforce standard aligned calculations:
      * Trailing PEG = Trailing PE (TTM) / Historical Net Profit CAGR (3Y CAGR)
      * Forward PEG = Forward PE / Expected Net Profit growth for the next 12 months
